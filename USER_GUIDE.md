## 🎯 What is VerifyAI?

VerifyAI is an intelligent search and research assistant that combines the power of web search with AI synthesis to give you **accurate, source-backed answers**. Unlike traditional search engines that return a list of links, or AI chatbots that might hallucinate facts, VerifyAI:

- ✅ **Searches the real web** for up-to-date information
- ✅ **Cites every claim** with numbered references to sources
- ✅ **Synthesizes information** from multiple sources into clear answers
- ✅ **Enables deep research** with multi-query exploration
- ✅ **Saves your research history** for future reference

### Who is VerifyAI for?

| User Type | Use Case |
|-----------|----------|
| **Students** | Research papers, fact-checking, learning new topics |
| **Researchers** | Literature review, trend analysis, data gathering |
| **Professionals** | Market research, competitor analysis, decision support |
| **Journalists** | Source verification, background research, fact-checking |
| **Curious Minds** | Exploring topics with verified, trustworthy information |

## 🔍 Quick Search Mode

Quick Search is the default mode, perfect for straightforward questions that need fast, reliable answers.

### How It Works

```
Your Question → Web Search → AI Synthesis → Cited Answer
```

1. **You ask a question** - Type naturally, like asking a friend
2. **Real-time web search** - VerifyAI searches the web for relevant sources
3. **AI analyzes sources** - The AI reads and understands the content
4. **Synthesized answer** - You receive a clear, comprehensive answer
5. **Citations included** - Every fact is backed by numbered references

### What You'll See

- **Answer Panel**: The main AI-generated response with inline citations
- **Sources Section**: Cards showing all websites consulted
  - Favicon and site name
  - Article title
  - Brief description
- **Follow-Up Questions**: Suggested questions to explore further

### Example

> **Your Question**: "What are the health benefits of green tea?"
>
> **VerifyAI Response**: "Green tea contains powerful antioxidants called catechins, particularly EGCG, which has been linked to improved brain function [1], fat burning [2], and reduced risk of heart disease [3]. Studies suggest that 3-5 cups daily provides optimal benefits [4]..."

## 🔬 Deep Research Mode

Deep Research is designed for comprehensive exploration of complex topics. It expands your query into multiple search angles and synthesizes a detailed research report.

### When to Use Deep Research

- 📚 Writing research papers or reports
- 🔄 Comparing multiple perspectives or options
- 📊 Analyzing trends or complex phenomena
- 🧪 Exploring new or specialized topics
- 📋 Creating comprehensive summaries

### How to Enable Deep Research

1. Click the **Deep Research** toggle in the search bar
2. Choose your **query count** (3-7 queries)
   - **3 queries**: Quick deep dive
   - **5 queries**: Balanced research (recommended)
   - **7 queries**: Exhaustive exploration
3. (Optional) Enable **Advanced Options**:
   - **Include Subpages**: Crawl linked pages for more context
   - **Force Fresh Results**: Get the latest data, bypass cached results
4. Submit your question

### The Research Process

When you submit a Deep Research query, you'll see real-time progress:

| Stage | Progress | What's Happening |
|-------|----------|------------------|
| **Expanding Queries** | 10% | AI generates diverse search angles from your question |
| **Searching Sources** | 20-80% | Each query searches the web in parallel |
| **Synthesizing Report** | 85% | AI analyzes all sources and writes comprehensive report |
| **Complete** | 100% | Final report ready with all citations |

### What You'll Receive

1. **Comprehensive Research Report**
   - Introduction and context
   - Multiple perspectives covered
   - Detailed findings with citations
   - Summary and conclusions

2. **Queries Explored Section**
   - Shows all search queries used
   - Indicates which sources came from which query
   - Original query marked with "Original" badge
   - Click to expand and see individual sources

3. **Enhanced Source Section**
   - More sources (20-30+ vs 6-8 in Quick Search)
   - Deduplicated (no repeated sources)
   - Click **Export PDF** to download full report

### Example Deep Research

> **Your Question**: "How does climate change affect ocean ecosystems?"
>
> **Expanded Queries Generated**:
> - "Climate change impact on coral reef ecosystems"
> - "Ocean acidification effects on marine life"
> - "Rising sea temperatures and fish migration patterns"
> - "Polar ice melt impact on ocean currents"
>
> **Result**: A 4000+ word comprehensive report covering all angles, with 25+ sources cited.

## 📊 Understanding Your Results

### Reading Citations

Citations appear as superscript numbers like `[1]`, `[2]`, `[3]` throughout the answer.

- **Single citation** `[1]`: Claim sourced from one article
- **Multiple citations** `[1][2]`: Claim verified by multiple sources
- **Reference section**: All sources listed at the bottom

### Source Cards

Each source card contains:
- **Favicon**: Website icon for quick recognition
- **Title**: Article or page title
- **Site Name**: Domain name (e.g., "nytimes.com")
- **Click to Open**: Opens source in new tab

### Queries Explored (Deep Research)

This section shows you exactly how VerifyAI researched your topic:

```
📊 Queries Explored                    4 queries • 28 sources
├─ 🔍 Your original question           Original | 8 sources  ▼
├─ 🔍 Expanded query 1                           | 7 sources  ▼
├─ 🔍 Expanded query 2                           | 6 sources  ▼
└─ 🔍 Expanded query 3                           | 7 sources  ▼
```

Click any row to expand and see the individual sources found for that query.

## 📁 Managing Your Research

### Chat History

Access your past research sessions from the sidebar:
- **Recent Chats**: Last 20 conversations
- **Titled by Topic**: Each session auto-titled based on content
- **Click to Resume**: Continue any previous research

### Bookmarks

Save important searches for quick access:

1. Find the **Bookmark** icon on any AI response
2. Click to save/unsave
3. Access all bookmarks from **Bookmarks** in the navigation
4. Your bookmarks include:
   - Original query
   - Full AI response
   - Timestamp

### PDF Export (Deep Research)

Download your research as a professional PDF document:

1. Complete a Deep Research query
2. In the **Sources** section, click **Export PDF**
3. The PDF includes:
   - Report title and generation date
   - Original query
   - Queries explored with source counts
   - Full research report
   - Complete source list with URLs

## 💡 Tips for Best Results

### Writing Effective Queries

| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Better |
|---------|----------|
| "Tell me about AI" | "What are the current limitations of large language models?" |
| "Climate change" | "How has global temperature changed over the last 50 years?" |
| "Best laptop" | "What laptop features matter most for software development in 2024?" |

### Quick Search vs Deep Research

| Scenario | Recommended Mode |
|----------|-----------------|
| "What year did X happen?" | Quick Search |
| "How does X work?" | Quick Search |
| "Compare X vs Y vs Z" | Deep Research |
| "Write a summary of X for a report" | Deep Research |
| "What are all the factors affecting X?" | Deep Research |

### Maximizing Deep Research

- **Be specific**: "Effects of microplastics on freshwater fish" > "Pollution effects"
- **Set appropriate query count**:
  - Use 3 for focused topics
  - Use 5-7 for broad or complex topics
- **Enable subpages** for in-depth articles
- **Use fresh results** for rapidly changing topics (news, trends)

## ❓ FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is VerifyAI free to use?**
> Currently, VerifyAI is available for free during the beta period. Usage limits may apply.

**Q: How current is the information?**
> VerifyAI searches the live web, so information is as current as what's published online. Enable "Force Fresh Results" in Deep Research for the absolute latest data.

**Q: Can I trust the AI's answers?**
> Every claim is backed by numbered citations. We encourage you to click through to sources to verify important information.

### Features

**Q: What's the difference between Quick Search and Deep Research?**
> Quick Search uses a single query for fast answers. Deep Research expands into 3-7 queries, searches more sources, and generates a comprehensive report.

**Q: How many sources does VerifyAI use?**
> - Quick Search: 6-8 sources
> - Deep Research: 20-35 sources (deduplicated)

**Q: Can I export my research?**
> Yes! In Deep Research mode, click "Export PDF" to download a formatted report with all sources.

### Technical

**Q: Why does the progress bar show during Deep Research?**
> Deep Research involves multiple steps: query expansion, parallel searching, and synthesis. The progress bar shows real-time status of each phase.

**Q: What happens if a source link is broken?**
> Source links are live web URLs. If a page is removed after VerifyAI found it, the link may not work. The original search was valid at the time.

**Q: Is my data private?**
> Your queries and research history are saved to your account only. We don't share or sell user data.
